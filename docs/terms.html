GameSphere is a Moonlight-based streaming client. You use it to stream software you already have the right to use from your PC.

1. The service

GameSphere helps you:

  • Discover and pair a Sunshine or Apollo host on your network
  • Browse a cover-art library (“The Sphere”) and stream as player 1
  • Optionally invite guests into a host session you start
  • Launch on-device iOS / emulator shortcuts you configure
  • Optionally subscribe to Shortcuts+ or buy Pro

GameSphere does not sell PC games, ROMs, or emulators. The free GameSphere Companion Tool (Companion) is a separate desktop utility.
